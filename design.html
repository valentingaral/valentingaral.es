Archive / 2008 to 2015

I used to
design.

I started my career as an industrial designer. At 22 I became Creative Director of Galería Mexicana de Diseño in Mexico City and ran my own studio, designing furniture, objects and spaces.

That work won awards, was exhibited in museums and published internationally. Then I realised the method mattered more to me than the object. I no longer design products; I design strategies. This archive stays here because it is where everything started.

Humilité

2015

Carpet / Pure Spanish wool / Andalucía

Limited edition carpet whose golden-ratio geometry evokes the faces of grandparents. Exhibited at CentroCentro, Madrid City Hall.

IBDF

2013

Identity & art direction / Mexico City

Identity for a design business fair, co-created with Franklin Asociados, built on classic wooden joints as a metaphor for industry alliances.

Animam

2013

Meditation cubicle / Burnt MDF / Mexico

A room for purifying the soul, commissioned by Masisa and Glocal Design Magazine, with MDF charred using the Japanese yakisugi technique.

Stilleven

2013

Wall desk, display & mirror / Walnut / Guadalajara

A piece for PECA that turns personal collections into a public still life: wool display, walnut desk and mirror in one object.

Salvum

2013

Home altar / Marble / Mexico

A contemporary altar for offerings of any faith, designed for Zafra, reclaiming the sacred corner that homes once had.

V100

2011

Vase / Recycled marble / Puebla

A numbered edition of 100 vases made from discarded quarry offcuts, a brutalist take on what luxury really means.

Banca Familiar

2011

Bench / Solid willow wood / Guadalajara

A hand-crafted bench for Le Porc-Shop, conceived as a meeting point to preserve family ties.

XXY

2010

Door handle series / Turned ash / France

Handles designed around people with disabilities as the starting point, claiming their right to a luxurious lifestyle.
